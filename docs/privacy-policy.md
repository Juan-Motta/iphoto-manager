# Privacy Policy: Easy Photo Saver

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Easy Photo Saver does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Your photos stay with you.** The app reads the photos and videos on an iPhone connected to your Mac with a cable, and copies them only to a folder on your Mac that you choose. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- **No account, no network.** The app has no sign-in and makes no network requests.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.
- **Local settings only.** The app remembers your thumbnail size on your Mac. It never leaves your Mac.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/Juan-Motta/iphoto-manager/issues.
